# Satellite hologram model credits

The GLB models below are bundled so the hologram works without a model-hosting service. NASA says its 3D-resource assets are free to download and use; follow NASA's media usage guidelines and do not imply NASA endorsement.

## NASA 3D Resources

These mission models are from the NASA 3D Resources repository: <https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources>. General catalog and use guidance: <https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/>.

- `iss.glb` — International Space Station, NASA VTAD.
- `hubble.glb` — Hubble Space Telescope, NASA.
- `jwst.glb` — James Webb Space Telescope, NASA.
- `tdrs.glb` — Tracking and Data Relay Satellites, NASA.
- `goes.glb` — Geostationary Operational Environmental Satellites, NASA.
- `landsat8.glb`, `aqua.glb`, `aura.glb`, `suomi-npp.glb`, `poes.glb`, `calipso.glb`, `icesat2.glb`, `fermi.glb`, `oco2.glb`, `sentinel6.glb`, `grace.glb`, `cygnss.glb`, `cloudsat.glb`, `radarsat1.glb`, `ssl1300.glb`, and `cubesat-2ru.glb` — NASA 3D Resources.
- `landsat9.glb` — NASA Landsat 9 3D model: <https://science.nasa.gov/mission/landsat/outreach/landsat-9-3d-model/>.
